No. Chapter 2 starts with zero assumptions. The product idea framework and validation process are specifically designed for first-time creators who don't yet know what to build.

The AI Income Engine covers the automation system — prompts, Make.com, email funnels. The Digital Product Blueprint covers the product creation and launch process specifically. The two complement each other; the Blueprint covers the product side more comprehensively.
Do I need a following or email list to implement this?
No. Chapter 4 covers launching without an audience specifically. The pre-launch strategy builds momentum in the two weeks before launch without requiring existing followers.

The validation framework in Chapter 2 significantly reduces this risk. If you complete validation and the product still doesn't sell, the data you collect tells you exactly what to change — price, description, or product angle.
