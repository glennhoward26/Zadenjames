Procrastination is not laziness. It is an emotional response to tasks that trigger overwhelm, fear, boredom, or uncertainty. This workbook fixes the root cause — not just the symptoms.

Overwhelm Trigger

Task feels too big. You do not know where to start. So you do not start at all.

Perfectionism Trigger

Fear that your output will not be good enough. The standard is so high it creates paralysis.

Fear Trigger

Avoiding the task because of what might happen. Fear of failure, judgement, or being exposed.

Unclear Trigger

You are not sure exactly what the task requires. Vagueness creates friction that stops action.
